# Next Train 

Next Train is a transit app with dedicated home screen widget and notifications, designed to give you a fast, glanceable view of trains departing your station on your regular train journey.

It’s especially useful for:

- daily commuting
- checking departure times before leaving home
- keeping an eye on the next few trains during the day
- switching between different routes depending on the time of day
- check the next trains for a route you care about without opening a full transit app

It is designed for commuters in:

- Victoria
- South Australia
- New South Wales
- Queensland
- Western Australia

### Home screen widget

Add Next Train to your home screen to see the next 3 trains for your chosen route.

### Sticky notification

Next Train can also show a sticky notification with the next 3 trains for your chosen route.

### Lock screen visibility

On compatible devices, the notification can also be shown on the lock screen.

### Scheduled visibility

You can set the notification to appear only during specific times and days, e.g. Monday-Friday @ 06:00-10:00.

This makes it easy to show the right route at the right time.

## How routes work

To add a route, choose:

- an origin and destination station (optional to narrow down station selection by choosing a line first)
- an optional custom name
- whether the route appears in the widget or notifications
- when the notification should be active, if notifications are enabled

Once set up, Next Train will show the next relevant departures for your saved route during the times you chose.

Note: Routes are meant to show departures that service both the origin and destination stations, so express services that bypass either station will not show.

## Good to know

- The app is focused on metro train networks
- It is intended for quick, glanceable commute information
- You can save more than one route if your travel needs change throughout the day
